# Services

I work with small and mid-sized businesses to build the software and integrations that help them run better.

Most businesses I work with have grown into a collection of tools and processes that were each reasonable on their own but never quite fit together. Data gets re-entered manually. Workflows live in someone's head. Software that worked fine a few years ago is starting to show its age. The work I do is about closing those gaps, not with the most sophisticated solution available, but with the right one given the time, budget, and people involved.

Before anything gets built, I start with a systems audit. That means sitting down with you, mapping what you have, and identifying where the highest-return changes are. Some of those are quick. Others take longer. Either way, you know what you're getting into before any work begins.

One thing I've learned working closely with directors and business owners is that a lot of the value in this kind of work comes down to communication. Translating what's technically possible into what actually makes sense for a business. Being honest about what's easy, what's hard, and what's going to take longer than it looks. That gap between expectation and reality is where a lot of projects go wrong, and closing it early is something I take seriously.

## What I Work On

### [Integrations & APIs](/services/integrations-apis)
Connecting your platforms, building the data flows between them, and designing the API surfaces that make your systems accessible to other tools. CRM platforms, phone systems, legacy applications, and anything else that needs to talk to anything else. That includes MCP servers that give AI systems access to your existing software.

### [Application Development](/services/application-development)
Custom software built around how your business actually works. Focused on smaller, well-scoped builds that replace a specific manual process or fill a gap that off-the-shelf tools can't cover. Not the right fit for large-scale systems that need a full team, but if the problem is contained and well-defined, it can probably be built.

### [AI & Automation](/services/ai-automation)
Workflow automation and AI-enriched pipelines that reduce manual work and surface information your team can act on. That includes building the systems, but also helping owners and teams understand what AI can actually do for their business. Most of the opportunity isn't where people expect to find it.
